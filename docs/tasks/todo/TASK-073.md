# TASK-073: Shoot locations: where to shoot, what it costs, what it is like
## Priority: MEDIUM | Added by the owner on 2026-10-13
## Files allowed: new lib/locations/*, app/locations/*, a new migration

## Why
New creators do not know where they may shoot, whether it is paid, how much,
how far it is, or what the place is like. Nothing on the platform answers that.

## What a location page holds
- Name, area, map pin, distance and drive time from the creator.
- Free or paid; price per hour/day; permit needed or not; who to contact.
- Photos (by creators who shot there), best light times, power, parking,
  toilets, safety notes, crowd level, indoor/outdoor.
- "Shot here" work from creators on the platform (links the portfolio).

## Plan
1. Seed: owner's own known spots + creators add places they have shot
   (reviewed before going live, like listing review in 072-listing-review).
2. Search and map: by area, distance, free/paid, type (studio, nature,
   urban, venue, rooftop).
3. Owners of paid places (studios, venues) can claim a page and take
   bookings later. Same payment path as gear; a commission line.
4. Tie-in: the booking flow can suggest a location with the creator, and the
   assistant can answer "free places to shoot near Soweto".

## Watch out
- Public places can still need a permit (city film offices). Say so; do not
  promise a place is legal to shoot.
- Never publish home addresses; private places show an area until booked.
