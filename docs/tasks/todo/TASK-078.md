# TASK-078: Shadow seats for beginners
## Priority: LOW | Added by the owner on 2026-10-13
## Files allowed: lib/crew/*, app/crew/*
## Depends on: TASK-074 (crew call)

## Why
Beginners cannot get work without credits and cannot get credits without
work. A junior seat on real shoots breaks that loop and grows the supply of
skilled crew.

## Plan
1. A crew call can add one shadow seat (optional, or encouraged by a lower fee).
2. Shadow seats are paid (a small fixed day rate), never free labour.
3. The lead on set rates the shadow afterwards: showed up, learned, helped.
4. Completed shadow days count as verified credits (TASK-079) with the
   label "shadow".
5. Simple matching: beginners near the shoot, in the role they want to learn.
